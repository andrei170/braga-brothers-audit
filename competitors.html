The Finding That Changes The Brief
You are not losing a crowded auction. There barely is one.

Your working assumption seems to be that Meta is not delivering because Meta does not work for roofing round here. What the evidence actually shows is that you are the only firm in Monmouth County treating it as a channel, and the others have not turned up.

That means the "Learn More" problem in your ads teardown is worth more than it looked, not less. A broken call-to-action in a crowded market costs you share. A broken call-to-action in an empty market costs you the whole market — you are paying to reach people nobody else is bidding for, and then asking them to read rather than book.

The Correction You Need To Hear
"Less than 2% of roofing contractors hold GAF Master Elite" is true nationally and nearly worthless locally
GAF Master Elite among the firms you actually compete with: Amore Homes (Marlboro) yes Garden State Roofing yes FB Roofing & Siding yes Braga Brothers yes Financing offered: all five firms we checked

Nationally it is a top-2% credential. In your own county it is table stakes, and so is financing. A homeowner comparing three quotes in Holmdel will see Master Elite on all three.

This qualifies the advice in your ads teardown. Lead with it against a chuck-in-a-truck operator, by all means — but it will not separate you from Amore or FB Roofing, because they say it too. Your separation has to come from something they cannot copy this month, and right now that is the channel itself.

Where You Are Genuinely Ahead
Four things that are true of you and not of them
  • 108 pages against 20, 26 and 4. Your site is substantially deeper than anything else in the county, and your city pages are real — we compared three of them and they share only 36–45% of their wording once you swap the town name out. That is written content, not a page farm.
  • You are the only one who can retarget. Nobody else has a pixel, so nobody else can advertise to a homeowner who looked at roofs last week and did not call. That is the cheapest audience in the channel and it is currently yours alone.
  • You are the only one measuring properly. Only one competitor has any analytics at all.
  • Your markup is done right — RoofingContractor and Organization types, FAQ schema, breadcrumbs, service and city markup. Only Amore and Little Town are in the same league, and neither has your page depth.
And where they are ahead

Garden State has been trading since 1970 and FB Roofing since 1972. Against your 30+ years, they will out-age you on a doorstep. That is worth knowing but not worth fighting — nobody wins an argument about who is older, and it is not what a homeowner with a leak is deciding on.

What We Could Not Check
Marked honestly rather than guessed
  • Your Google Business Profile, and theirs. You mentioned a company managing your profile without results — map pack rankings, review counts and review velocity are the things that would answer that, and we have no reliable way to measure them. It is the single biggest gap in this document and we are not going to guess at it.
  • Anyone's spend, reach, results, lead volume, close rate or revenue, including yours. The Ad Library discloses creative and launch dates only.
  • Whether any of these firms advertise on Google, which is a different auction and one we have not assessed.
  • Tracking loaded by JavaScript after rendering on the two firms without a tag container we could read.
  • Every roofing firm in Monmouth County. This is the set we could find and verify, and it excludes national operators and storm-chasers working the area.

You are first into a channel nobody else has entered. That is a rare position and it is not permanent.

The fix is the button, not the market.
